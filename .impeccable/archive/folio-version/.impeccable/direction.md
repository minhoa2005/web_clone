# Approved direction: annotated historical folio

The user approved implementation of docs/REDESIGN_PLAN.md on 15 October 2026. Build path: code-led; no image comp is the fidelity target.

## First viewport

Vietnamese historical narrative on warm ivory at left; a quiet dark olive geographic canvas at right; a chronological dock below. A real selected year, event title, summary, geographic location and next action are visible. The header identifies the project and exposes contents and source evidence. The globe is the spatial explanation of the selected record, not a travel visualization.

## Product requirements

- Vietnamese students and Vietnamese-first copy.
- Six learning periods within the textbook's three chapters; coverage ends at the 2021 edition.
- Timeline, reading, selected places and browser links derive from one event.
- Every record has cited textbook page images or an identified institutional supplement.
- Geometry is contemporary orientation and must not assert historical borders.
- Remove continuous grain, pulses, routes and rotation; lazy-load 3D and pause at rest.
- Support 2D and readable content without WebGL; source/index drawers have native modal focus handling.

## Visual system

Paper #f3eddf; reading surface #faf6ec; ink #2c2922; red #922e2b; muted #70695d; rule #d8cfbd. Globe field #1b2420; land #64705a; geographic emphasis #e7cf9c.

Self-hosted Noto Serif for headings/year; Be Vietnam Pro for reading and controls. Opaque surfaces, fine rules, square controls and a book-derived mark. No generated imagery or decorative particles. Page reproductions are identified as textbook pages, not event photographs.

## Signature interaction

Select a dated stop, then read the account while the globe focuses once and settles. A short native camera transition respects reduced motion. Previous/next, six periods, and an accent-insensitive search provide alternatives to dragging.
